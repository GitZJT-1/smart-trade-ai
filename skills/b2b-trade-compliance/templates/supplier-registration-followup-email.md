# 供应商注册 — 跟进邮件模板

> 配合 b2b-trade-compliance Phase 7 使用。发送前替换 [占位符]。

## 模板一：提交后第 7 个工作日跟进（无回复时）

**Subject:** Supplier Registration Follow-up — [Company Legal Name]

```
Dear [Customer] Supply Chain Management Team,

I am writing to follow up on our supplier registration submitted through
your [New Supplier form / supplier portal] on [date].

Company: [Company Legal Name]
Contact: [Name], [Title]
Email: [company email]

We specialize in [product lines — e.g. metallurgical equipment and spare
parts, custom-machined non-standard parts per customer drawings]. Our
in-house process chain covers [casting, heat treatment, CNC machining,
assembly].

We would be glad to provide any additional information required for the
evaluation — company profile, [ISO 9001] certificate, equipment lists or
reference cases from our customers.

Could you kindly let us know the status of our registration and whether
there are any further steps required on our side?

Thank you for your time. We look forward to the opportunity to support
[Customer]'s projects.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Title]
[Company]
Phone: [phone]
Email: [company email]
Website: [website]
```

## 模板二：收到供应商门户（Oracle/Ariba）邀请后回复

**Subject:** Supplier Portal Registration — [Company Legal Name]

```
Dear [Customer] Supply Chain Management Team,

Thank you for inviting us to complete the supplier registration through
your supplier portal.

We have received the invitation and will complete the registration as
requested. Please note the following contact person for our company:

Administrative Contact:
- Name: [Name]
- Title: [Title]
- Department: [Department]
- Email: [company email]
- Phone: [phone]

Should any additional documents (company profile, [ISO 9001] certificate,
bank details) be required, please let us know and we will provide them
promptly.

Thank you for the opportunity. We look forward to a successful
cooperation.

Best regards,
[Name], [Title]
[Company]
```

## 收件人方案（官网无公开采购邮箱时）

| 方案 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| A. 邀请邮件原线程回复 | 最准；直接用客户发来的邮件回复 |
| B. LinkedIn 建联采购/SCM 团队 | 先 InMail/加好友，拿到采购负责人邮箱后再发 |
| C. 官网公开邮箱 | 仅当官网明示采购/供应商邮箱时用；营销/社媒邮箱不对口采购 |

## 发送前自检

- [ ] 收件人邮箱已确认（非编造/猜测）
- [ ] 必填数据真实（无占位符残留）
- [ ] 缩写首次出现给出全称（Phase 2）
- [ ] 贸易术语带 Incoterms 2020 + 地点（Phase 3）
- [ ] 语气专业、不卑不亢、无过度承诺
